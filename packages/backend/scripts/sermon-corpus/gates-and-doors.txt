Gates and Doors

Every life has gates and doors.

This is the mystery of operation and stagnation that a man can be moving physical but in the realm of the spirit the gates has not yet opened and you can forever languish in stagnation

Acts 12:9-10 -They passed the first and second guards and came to the iron gate leading to the city. It opened for them by itself, and they went through it. When they had walked the length of one street, suddenly the angel left him.

There are Angels assigned that are commissioned to open the gates of men.

There are gates that leads to territories. If there are gates, then there are watchers and gatekeepers

The gates of your finances, health, marriage can be closed.

The Bible calls human beings a house, even the demons call a person a house. Houses have gates. If gates are not attended, gates can be trespassed.

The moment you dishonor men you close doors
