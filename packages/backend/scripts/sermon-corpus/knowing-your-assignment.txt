Knowing your Assignment

There is forever established an assignment for you.   
  
An assignment is a special work given to someone by another which need to be done and completed within a given period of time.   
  
God is a master planner who knows about what need to be done and by who within which time.   
  
Every man was fashioned in an intricate manner and endowed with every quality by God to fulfill very specific assignments at the appointed time. Not only did God create men with these qualities, he also wisely placed each one of us at the right place on earth/ environment to enable man carry out his assignment   
  
Esther was created to become the salvation of the Jews. To enable her carry out her divine assignment, God orchestrated everything to fit in the assignment of Esther, by making her the Queen.   
  
The assignment of God is paramount to our passion. Jesus prayed and said ' Father, if you are willing, take this cup from me; yet not my will, but yours be done' Luke 22:42   

  
SUCCEEDING WHERE OTHERS HAVE FAILED:   
Romans 8:29-30(MSG) God knew what he was doing from the very beginning. He decided from the outset to shape the lives of those who love him along the same lines as the life of his Son.

The Son stands first in the line of humanity he restored. We see the original and intended shape of our lives there in him. After God made that decision of what his children should be like, he followed it up by calling people by name.

After he called them by name, he set them on a solid basis with himself.

And then, after getting them established, he stayed with them to the end, gloriously completing what he had begun. 31-39 So, what do you think? With God on our side like this, how can we lose? If God didn’t hesitate to put everything on the line for   
us,   
  
Jesus knew that God's assignment over his life was way greater than his desires.

The biggest challenge faced by many is about knowing what is their assignment. This question has at many times lingered in the hearts of many but most of the time it does not have a definate answer.   
  
How do I get to know my divine assignment?   
  
There are many thoughts and feelings that floods our mind when we try to answer or find the meaning to this question. But the truth is, the answer is found in Him who gave the assignment to each one of us!   
  
If we go into him, God, and ask him;   
  
why am I here today?   
  
Why was I created as I am?   
  
He is a good God and he will reveal your assignment to you.   
  
Many people are caught up by their needs and wants such that they are always running after them with such commitment until they become carried away to believe that that is their divine assignment. But in the real truth, they are far away from their assignments.   
  
Being busy fulfilling many small and big agendas may not mean that you are pursuing your God given assignment. It may mean that you have become a slave to your routine which has enslaved you from pursuing God given assignment.   
  
Yet some have been entrusted with activities which are if the physical nature and if done they reveal the mysteries of heaven in a magnitude of God's glory.   
  
Many times, we tend to believe that what we are currently doing is enough to fulfill the assignment.
