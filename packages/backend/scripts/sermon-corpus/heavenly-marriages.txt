Heavenly Marriages

Heavenly Marriages

In the 21st Century, the institution of marriage has been buttered heavily by many issues. The modern progressive lifestyle has stretched the institution of marriage in limits never seen before.

Many families have been left shuttered when their marriage failed to hold. Fathers and mothers have been devastated beyond words and children have been left to wander on unguided terrains of harsh world.

In spite of all these, there are families that have stood the test of time and character. These families have become the pillars of many generations and the hope of humanity.

This book aims at helping those who desire to understand the dynamics of marriage in order to overcome the challenges and embrace the wisdom of how to live together in harmony.

The right attitude.

Unfortunately, this is not the reality of what is being experienced by many who are married. The state of the couples is of uncertainty. Each of the couple waylay the other expecting him/her to mess up and he/she will be blamed for ever!

But much of these problems is not in the challenges that come over couples and families. The problem is the ATTITUDE that one has towards the other.

Those who have a positive attitude towards each other tends to see a challenging situation as a test of time rather than a messy destructive situation.

Couples who have a positive attitude for each other resolves their situation with love and care knowing so well that they will still be together after the situation.

When a couple have a positive attitude towards each other, and they love one another truly, that couple can go through any situation together and emerge more stronger and better than they were there before.

THE POWER OF RELATIONSHIPS.

Have you ever found yourself in a WhatsApp group and you are expected to contribute for a baby shower, a wedding, a dowry party etc and you wonder, who gave out your number? Have you ever received a call and someone need help and the last time that person had called was 5 years ago when the same person needed help? As a pastor people come for counseling and pastoral help and you wonder, where were they all those times?

My point is simple, cultivate your Fellowships and Friendships and stop waiting for your day of need and you are busy looking for people to help you out. Sow seeds of Friendships with people and your time of need they will come to help without feeling forced. Your friends are your future and Solomon said, a person with friends must show himself friendly. Your Networks are your Networth and God uses people to bless People and so develop the right Fellowships and Friendships and you will eat the Fruit.

We have other people who seem independent and self contained but it's a lie, it's deception to think that you don't need others. People are Important than anything else, forgive even the difficult for shall need them one day. I have seen families struggling to look for a pastor to bury their loved ones, the reason is because when the person was alive, he or she was not committed to any Fellowship.

Don't wait for your hour of need, to rush out seeking for help, sow seeds of love, support others, bless others, visit others and God will make sure it comes back to you.

God will use people to open your doors, so always smile at people even in your low moods, sow seeds of Friendships and in the day of need it shall be well.

Raise Friends before you Raise Funds.

Keep yourself connected to people, reconnect with those you disconnected and pay the reconnection fee and in the day of trouble you won't sink into the pit since you have enough Support base to raise you up and Support you.
