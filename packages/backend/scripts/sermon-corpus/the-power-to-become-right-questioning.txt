**The power of questioning** 

It seems when people question, they don't go deep enough. They deliberately ignore the answers that will serve them.

  

**Great questions = great answers**

  

Indeed, the power of questioning is a transformative one. It's a tool for deepening our understanding, expanding our perspective, and stimulating our curiosity. 

  

When we ask the right questions, we can shape the answers we receive, and by extension, our actions, choices, and outcomes.

  

But as you've noted, many people don't fully leverage this power. They may ask superficial questions, avoid difficult questions, or neglect to question their assumptions or beliefs. This can result in a limited understanding and missed opportunities for growth or change.

  

**To get more from right questions, we can:**

**Go deeper:** Instead of stopping at the first answer, we can ask follow-up questions to gain a more nuanced understanding. For example, instead of just asking "What happened?", we can also ask "Why did it happen? How did it happen? What were the consequences? How could it have been prevented or done better?"

  

**Embrace discomfort:** The best questions often lead to uncomfortable truths. Instead of avoiding these, we can embrace them as opportunities for growth. This involves being honest with ourselves and willing to confront our blind spots and biases.

  

**Ask open-ended questions:** Instead of asking questions that can be answered with a simple yes or no, we can ask open-ended questions that encourage more thoughtful and detailed responses.

  

**Challenge our assumptions**: Often, our questions are based on certain assumptions. By questioning these assumptions, we can uncover new possibilities and perspectives.

**Be genuinely curious:** Authentic curiosity leads to deeper, more meaningful questions. Instead of asking questions just to get an answer or to appear smart, we can ask questions to truly understand and learn.

  

Ultimately, the power of questioning lies in our willingness to be open, curious, and receptive. By fully engaging with this power, we can unlock deeper insights, make more informed decisions, and foster greater growth and transformation.
