BE TRANSFORMED BY THE RENEWAL OF YOUR MIND ROMANS 12:2

Introduction:   
The key to learning is to sit down and the key to speed is to wait.

Going hurriedly does not mean speed and seeking here and there does not mean learning.   
  
God is a God who reveals truths that are 'mysteries' to the ordinary but great treasure to the called through a process called transformation.

For you to operate effectively in the Kingdom, you need to move from one glory to the other. The move occurs when there is a transformation shift that occur in our mindset through the correct grasping of the word of truth.

  
  
When your mind is transformed to a particular level, you are ushered into a different dimension of reality commensurate to the degree of your transformation.   
  
Transformation does not happen by coincidence or by chance. Transformation is developed, it's initiated by learning the truths of the word of God.   
  
Truth in itself may not free you. But it needs to be sequentially arranged. The transformation happens by the change of your mindset from one dimension of truth to another dimension of truth.   
  
For a true transformation to occur, you not only align yourself with a particular set of truth but you embrace the truth such that you become one in the truth.   
  
Romans 12:2   
  
And do not be conformed to this world [any longer with its superficial values and customs], but be transformed and progressively changed [as you mature spiritually] by the renewing of your mind [focusing on godly values and ethical attitudes], so that you may prove [for yourselves] what the will of God is, that which is good and acceptable and perfect [in His plan and purpose for you].   
  
  
Don’t become so well-adjusted to your culture that you fit into it without even thinking. Instead, fix your attention on God. You’ll be changed from the inside out. Readily recognize what he wants from you, and quickly respond to it. Unlike the culture around you, always dragging you down to its level of immaturity, God brings the best out of you, develops well-formed maturity in you.

We are perfectly useless as Christ-exalting Christians if all we do is conform to the world around us. And the key to not wasting our lives with this kind of success and prosperity, Paul says, is being transformed. “Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your minds.”

The word “transformed” is used one time in all the gospels, namely, about Jesus on the mountain of transfiguration (the mountain of “transformation” — same word, metemorphōthē): “And he was transfigured before them, and his face shone like the sun, and his clothes became white as light” (Matthew 17:2; Mark 9:2).

  
  
- conforming is a change from outside to the inside. You become like the FLUID inside a container. You take the shape of the container.   
- transformation is the change that happens from inside out. You become the influencer of your environment and situations.   
  
There is nothing as powerful as a transformed life: Do not stand at the path of a transformed mind for it can do all things that it set out to do.   
- When a mind is transformed, it has such a capacity to attract things that are mind boggling.   
- A transformed mindset holds the ability and the power to retain all things gotten by understanding. If you cannot keep something, the thing was not gotten by understanding.   
  
  
The power of a transformed life:   
Genesis 11:4-6   
Then they said, “Come, let’s build ourselves a city and a tower that reaches Heaven. Let’s make ourselves famous so we won’t be scattered here and there across the Earth.” God came down to look over the city and the tower those people had built. God took one look and said, “One people, one language; why, this is only a first step. No telling what they’ll come up with next—they’ll stop at nothing! Come, we’ll go down and garble their speech so they won’t understand each other.”   
  
- A transformed mind has a level of power commensurate to their level of transformation.   
- A transformed mind taps into the already established principles of the word of God at the dimension of transformation.
