GATE KEEPERS

Who are the gatekeepers?

Gatekeepers are people or spirits that man the doors (entrance or exit) of a building, city or a country. For cities and countries, many times we call them entry and exit points.

We have different types of gatekeepers in the hierarchy of governance starting from simple gatekeepers like watchmen to the senior gatekeepers like officials in the governments.

In the physical realm/ dimension, there principalities, powers, rulers of the darkness of this world, and spiritual wickedness in high places whereas in the spiritual dimension there are principalities, powers, rulers of the darkness of this world, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms.

Ephesian 6:12 - For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the powers of this dark world and against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms.

Two types of gatekeepers

There are two types of Gate keepers –

  - Godly gatekeepers
  - Demonic gatekeepers.

These two types of gatekeepers’ manifest in the physical dimension and as well as in the spiritual dimension.

Godly gatekeepers are all who have received Jesus Christ as their Lord and saviour and who have understood their place in the purposes of God.

Demonic gatekeepers are all other people who are not born again, like witches, diviners, astrologers, people who use witch craft to sustain their livelihoods etc
