Change your mindset, Learn, unlearn and relearn.

Mark 2:22 And who would pour new wine into an old wineskin? Eventually the wine will ferment and make the wineskin burst, losing everything—the wine will be spilled and the wineskin ruined. Instead, new wine is always poured into new wineskins.”

Genesis 13:14-17

The Lord said to Abram after Lot had parted from him,

  - Why do you always hold on your Lot?
  - Why do you always hold the things of the past?
  - What are your fears?

“Look around from where you are, to the north and south, to the east and west.

  - Our mindset dictates the lenses that we use to see
  - Our Mindset dictates our choices
  - Our Mindset creates our reality.
  - Our Mindset directs our pursuit
  - Our mindset lays hold or scatter our destiny

Romans 12:2b but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

  - Shed off your old mindset
  - Let go of the fears and doubt of the Past
  - Let Go of friends and relatives that pull you down
  - Learn unlearn and relearn.
  - Let New possibilities come in
  - Surround yourself with people who will challenge you upwards

Hebrew 11:1-2 Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen. 2 For by it the elders obtained a good report.

For Us to be able to obtain the Good report, we need.

  - Constantly work on our mindset
  - Keep adding value - valuable information/ Skills in us
  - Keep allowing changes from the manufacture to be updated
  - Keep evolving through the revelation and
  - Keep the transformation

All the land that you see I will give to you and your offspring forever. I will make your offspring like the dust of the earth, so that if anyone could count the dust, then your offspring could be counted.

  - Your purpose is hidden in your understanding of Gods will
  - Your Destiny is in getting aligned with the will of the father.

17 Go, walk through the length and breadth of the land, for I am giving it to you.”
