Understanding times and seasons of prophesies: The rapture

We are living in times when the church is so lost in the culture of luke-warmness, when the

church is so busy speaking about success and tips to successful living and they have forgotten

that we are sojourners headed for heaven.

The church today does not understand this wonderful revelation of Jesus about times and

seasons of his coming.

Two types of coming

1. When Jesus will come in the glory of the Father

Revelation 1:7 Look He is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see him, even those

who pierced him will mourn.

Mathew 16:27: For the Son of Man is going to come in his Father’s glory with his angels,

and then he will reward each person according to what they have done.

Isaiah 40:5 And the glory of the LORD will be revealed, and all people will see it together.

For the mouth of the LORD has spoken.”

2. When Jesus will come like a thief

Revelation 16:15 - “Look, I come like a thief! Blessed is the one who stays awake and

remains clothed, so as not to go naked and be shamefully exposed.” Mathew 24:42

“Therefore keep watch, because you do not know on what day your Lord will come.

This is where I want us to get an understanding and then in the coming video, we shall speak

about his second coming.

There are two categories of people in the church. Mathew 25:1-13

1. Those who are ready and waiting for Jesus – 5 wise virgins - which is the church

2. Those who will not be ready – the 5 foolish virgins – which is the world

The world does not understand these information

John 1:10 He was in the world, and though the world was made through him, the world did

not recognize him. 11 He came to that which was his own, but his own did not receive him.

• The world does not need to know the time because they don’t have the information

about Jesus coming.

• The world of Noah did not understand the information of the flood. They did not

perceive the warnings Mathew 24:36-39

• The church needs to understand the times because they have a date with Jesus! Jesus

gave the church signs of his coming - Mathew 24: 4-7

In the rapture, people will be going on with their business as usual but

Jesus says in Mathew 24 vs 37 But as the days of Noah were, so shall also the coming of the Son

of man be. For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking,

marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark, and knew not until

the flood came, and took them all away; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.

This is what will happen in the rapture:

Then shall two be in the field; the one shall be taken, and the other left.

Two women shall be grinding at the mill; the one shall be taken, and the other left. Watch

therefore: for ye know not what hour your Lord doth come.

Pauls says about the rapture and in relation to those who have gone ahead – 1 Thessalonians

4:13 – Brothers and sisters, we do not want you to be uninformed about those who sleep in

death, so that you do not grieve like the rest of mankind, who have no hope. 14 For we believe

that Jesus died and rose again, and so we believe that God will bring with Jesus those who have

fallen asleep in him. 15 According to the Lord’s word, we tell you that we who are still alive, who

are left until the coming of the Lord, will certainly not precede those who have fallen

asleep. 16 For the Lord himself will come down from heaven, with a loud command, with the

voice of the archangel and with the trumpet call of God, and the dead in Christ will rise

first. 17 After that, we who are still alive and are left will be caught up together with them in the

clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And so we will be with the Lord forever. 18 Therefore

encourage one another with these words.

Revelation 20:1-6 The ready church will be caught up in Christ and they will go with him to reign

for 1000 years what we call millennium

When Jesus comes and take the church with him,

1. The world will not know about his coming – only those who are prepared for Jesus will

go with him

2. Those who are not prepared will be stricken by the sad reality that they have been left

behind

3. He will not take those who thinks that they are ready and yet not ready, not those who

looks like ready and not those who were ready but now they are not ready.

And this are signs of his coming: Mathew 24:3-7

Are you prepared?
