There are several ways that God speak to us.

1.  Through his word: The first major avenue that God uses to speak to us is through his word.
2.  Through the witness of the Holy Spirit inside you – God will give you a particular direction; an impression in your heart.
3.  Situational way – things/issues may Arise, people may be speaking their own things and you happen to listen and God speak to you.
4.  Dreams and the vision of the night. Job 33:14-16 - For God speak once, yea twice, yet man perceive it not. In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep fall upon men, in slumbering upon the bed; Then he opens the ears of men, and seal their instruction.

Dreams

One of the most misunderstood phenomena is the one of the dreams. Dreams are expressions that are symbolic. They come in the form of metaphors and images.

Dreams are a communication channel between the spirits in the spiritual realm and man in the physical realm.

Dreams are a gift from a loving Father and are intended to draw you into an intimate relationship with Him.

God uses dreams to align our hearts, thoughts and intentions to His eternal purpose.

When it comes to dreams, there are four categories of people

1.  Those who do not dream.
2.  Those who do not have the clarity of dreams / remembering all dreams clearly.
3.  Those who dreams and fails to know how to interpret dreams.
4.  Those who dreams, remember the dreams and are able to interpret the dreams. These are blessed people who dream and understand the meaning of the dreams.

Things to note:

1.  Dreams are very key in man’s life.
2.  Dreams come to all, born again and not — dreams are part of men both born again and not born again. Pharaoh dreamt and Joseph dreamt too.
3.  The Angels are the deliverer of dreams.
4.  Dreams and angelic ministry go hand in hand.
5.  Dreams can open and lay open your entire world.
6.  When you see a repetitive dream – this is emphasis.

Types of dreams

Cares of life Dreams - Ecclesiastes 5:3a - A dream comes when there are many cares

Chemical Dreams - Dreams that come due to Medication and Drug abuse

Spiritual Dreams – Dreams that Originate from either God or from Satan and his demons

Spiritual Dreams

1.  Instructional dreams / Directional dreams: Matthew 1:20 – In the dream God gave Joseph the direction that was right to take.
2.  Warning dreams: Matthew 2:13 - The birth of Jesus – The angels gave Joseph a photographic picture; very definite dream of what to do. When you get the warning dreams, do not take it lightly.
3.  Revelatory dreams – There are dreams that reveals what happened in the past as well as the things that will happen in the future.

Spiritual Transactions in the dream

There are many spiritual transactions that happen in dreams. May God give you a divine strategy in your place of business. God did not create you to struggle all through your life. God desires to bless you.

You can receive a mantle through a dream. A ‘mantle’ is a Scriptural metaphor (symbol) for a calling, ministry, anointing and—when applicable—office, given to individuals by God.

Evil Transactions

Job 36:20 - Desire not the night, when people are cut off in their place.

Matthew 13:25 - but while men slept, the enemy came and sowed tares among the wheat and went his way.

There are great things that God reveal to us in dreams but these great things do not come to be fulfilled. The lack of them being fulfilled does not make God a liar, rather, they reveal the lack of wisdom from men to understand how spiritual affairs are conducted.

Sexual dreams.

Sexual dreams are not of God. These are Demonic forces assigned to destroy marital life and progress.

Characteristics of evil dreams

1.  You wake up very shaken
2.  You see Demonic spirits contending with you
3.  You are horrified to sleep

What causes evil dreams?

1.  They are a product of evil altars.
2.  Habitual sin – sin is a hedge breaker.
3.  Covenants – if in your life or family there is a Demonic covenant, that will open way to Demonic dreams.

DOORS THAT ALLOWS GODLY DREAMS

Our relationship / walk with God. Our understanding of God’s ways, laws and statutes. Having the Holy Spirit in our lives. Your thought patterns – whatever we feed our mind.

Interpretation of dreams

Every dreamer also has the power to interpret the dream. The power to interpret the dreams comes from the Holy Spirit.

Disclaimer: You have to seek God for every dream. Dreams do not have a common meaning. Meanings are given by God.

Major Symbols: Trees, Sheep, Child, Presidents and Kings, Stars, Bridegroom, Cows, Mountains – Government and authorities, Car/vehicle/aeroplane / moving object that need to be directed means ministry, business etc.
