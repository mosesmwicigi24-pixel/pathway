Hebrew 11:1, Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see. This is what the ancients were commended for.   
  
There is great certainty on people who sees with their inner eyes beyond the visible horizons that are seen by our physical eyes and hears with their inner ears the motivational cheers comes from heavens. These type of people are called visionaries.   
  
If every Christian resonates with the same frequency as with God's word, each is given an extra ability to see and to hear beyond the ordinary, way above the the earthly and into the guaranteed voices of Gods promises that availed much.   
  
God's way of working is not only on the revealed ok and the seen but greatly on the revelation of tomorrow and the unseen. The unseen are revealed by faith in God's word. The revealed unseen is made manifest by his promises and attained by the walk of faith in against the seen and the tangible.   
  
God entrusts the riches of beyond the horizons to those who dare trust him and walk on the water like Peter, hit the busting rivers with the cloak like Elisha, walk on the deserts like Elijah, hung on the cross and die for a noble cause like Jesus!   
  
Those who choose to close the deserts and dangerous forests on their way to their destiny that is beyond the horizon requires the determination and courage which is not obtained from men on earth but that which is supplied by Jesus Christ. They do not sit down and seek to be ministered by men but they are ministered by angels of God like Elijah.   
  
  
The Dangers of staying on this side of the horizon   
1. On this side of the visible horizon, there is full of limitation of purpose. God has put in us all necessary ingredients of greatness, of fruitfulness and of flourishing. But all this are not readily percieved by careful looking through the common understanding, but through God's wisdom that is revealed to the faithful and courageous and to those who choose to hear God speak.   
2. Mediocre success - The kind of success that is achieved by those who looks out to the visible horizons as the destination of their purpose, the kind of achievement for those who looks at the faded blue lines of their weak gaze as the prize of their living will only harvest vain glory. Their success is vanity and short-lived. This kind of success is temporal and withers like the grass of the field or evaporates like the morning due.   
3. Fatigue and dispare - Those who walk by what they simply see and perceive quickly finds out that what they see and perceive is not able to sustain them. Rather, they grow weary and faint. They die and are forgotten as quickly as they came.   
  
  
The blessings of looking and seeing over the horizon   
1. They do not walk alone but they walk with the one who is beyond the horizon.   
2. They don't try to figure out the path to their destiny, but they trend on paths that have been made straight by God towards their destiny.   
3. They do not struggle to gaze about their destiny for they can see beyond the ordinary.   
4. They do not seek men's approval for they have obtained the approval from heaven.   
5. They do not walk in the confusion of self realization but they cherish a purpose driven lifestyle given to them by the everlasting God.   
6. They are not influenced by the shortsightedness of a generation for they are influencers.   
7. They speak with the confidence of the eternal wisdom extended to them by him who is at the end of the beginning.
