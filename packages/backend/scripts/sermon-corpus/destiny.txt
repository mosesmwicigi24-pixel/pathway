Destiny

The greatest loss in a man other than missing heaven is missing his or her destiny. We are here for a season and for a reason and the value of your season of being is in understanding your reason of being.

You are not a tourist on earth but a heaven sent legislator, an ambassador of Christ and a kingdom agent.

The joy of life is revealed when you discover your identity, walk in purpose and you fulfill your destiny.

Premature death is not dying young but dying with un utilised potential.

Pursue your destiny

1 Samuel 30:7- 8

Then David said to Abiathar the priest, the son of Ahimelek, “Bring me the ephod.” Abiathar brought it to him, and David inquired of the Lord , “Shall I pursue this raiding party? Will I overtake them?” “Pursue them,” he answered. “You will certainly overtake them and succeed in the rescue.”

How to pursue your Destiny

To pursue your destiny, you need to:

1.  Know God: Hebrew 12:2 Looking unto Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith,
2.  Enquire from the Lord - we should always enquire from the Lord
3.  Collect yourself up - when you are beaten up, collect yourself up.
4.  Plan the battle and create the formation.

When you are out of your destiny,

1. There is frustration.

2. There is pain and despair. At this point, you make many wrong decisions that hurt you.

3. There is brokenness - you become full if remorse and hatred

4. There is loss of direction - you become helpless to yourself and to others

Destroyers of Destiny

THE REUBEN WITHOUT A LEGACY

Genesis 49:3,4 - Reuben, you are my firstborn, my might.....unstable as the waters, you will not excel

Facts about Reuben

1.  Reuben was the firstborn of Jacob. He was supposed to inherit the double portion blessing as the firstborn son. The favor to inherit and become great was his.
2.  He was born with potential and promise to become prominent and powerful but he became a disappointment.

Reuben could not honor his father he committed adultery with his father's concubine - AVOID THE ISSUES THAT CAN DEFILE YOUR DESTINY. Because of this he was meant to be blessed but he was cursed.

Reuben had emotional problems, he could not control his appetites - SELF-CONTROL IS A SIGN OF SUCCESSFUL PEOPLE.

Reuben could not tame his flesh - DISCIPLINE IS ONE OF THE INGREDIENT THAT TAKES YOU TO YOUR DESTINY.

Reuben was unstable, unpredictable and indecisive.

Reuben could not decide on important issues, he wavered - Judges 5:15,16. When Deborah was mobilizing the tribes of Israel to go for battle, the tribe of Reuben could not make up their mind - YOUR DESTINY IS DETERMINED BY THE DECISIONS YOU MAKE ESPECIALLY IN TIMES OF CRISIS.

The tribe of Reuben stayed by the campfires warming themselves when the other tribes were risking their lives in battle. Reuben loved Comforts and Convinience more than Conflict and Crisis - SOME COMFORTS CAN CAUSE COMPROMISE. Because of this, the tribe of Reuben never produced any king, priest, judge, prophet or any prominent person in Israel.

Reuben was born great but died unrecognized and uncelebrated and left no legacy. If you want to realize your potential, then avoid the mistakes and the weaknesses of Reuben.

Six battles of destiny

There are six battles that every man with a great destiny will face

1. Battle of location

2. Battle of name/identity

3. Battle of diet/Appetites

4. Battle of language

5. Battle of knowledge

6. Battle of worship

These are the areas that Nebuchadnezzar laboured to interfere with while dealing with Daniel and His battalion. If the enemy manages to tamper with the six areas, your life is doomed.

When your identity is affected, your purpose is distorted and your destiny is aborted.
