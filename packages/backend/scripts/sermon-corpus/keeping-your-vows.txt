Vows and Sacrifices

Many people have gotten a great revelation and understanding concerning vows and their effects over the destinies of men, and many more have lacked this knowledge and they have ended ruining their destinies.

Psalm 50:5. “Gather to me this consecrated people, who made a covenant with me by sacrifice.”

God is pleased by the people who makes and keeps their vows. Vows are commitment of our own lives to God.

What is a vow?

A vow is a voluntary promise or promises which are made between a person and God or even between people. A vow is a show of commitment to God.

A vow is a solemn promise made to God to perform or to abstain from performing a certain thing.

The issue of vows are not to be taken rightly.

We make vows through:

There are people who vow to God because they are desperate and they need God to come through for them. Others make vows due to a desire to grow from one level to the other. And yet others make vows because they want to prove a point.

We should make vows when we well thought of.

  - Prayer and fasting
  - Giving gifts as a sign of vow
  - Praying for a child by making a vow
  - Tithes should be considered outrightly - leta zaka Kamili.

Many people when faced with difficult situations, they tend to find any means possible to get out of the problem. This include making vows to God, committing to Him several conditions that if he meet those conditions, they are going to do one thing or the other. How many people have made vows to God?

The Bible goes to a long length to warn us about making vows that are non-intentional or not well thought of. This is because many people made their vows to God and they failed to honor those vows.

The failure to honor their vows made God angry over those people. He left them and took with Him his presence.

The presence of God carries:

  - The power of God,
  - Favor of God -
  - Blessings of God
  - Security of God

Importance of making vow to God

1.  It pleases God when we make a vow to him and we fulfil it - It's a sign of commitment to him
2.  God is fully committed to fulfil his word (His part of the vow) Jeremiah 1: 12 - The Lord said to me, “You have seen correctly, for I am watching to see that my word is fulfilled.”
3.  Keeping our vows to God give us victory over our enemies - Judges 11:30-31 And Jephthah made a vow to the Lord: “If you give the Ammonites into my hands, 31 whatever comes out of the door of my house to meet me when I return in triumph from the Ammonites will be the Lord’s, and I will sacrifice it as a burnt offering.”
4.  Making vows makes us responsible Christian's and sensitive to the word of God
5.  A vow is like a catalyst that activates our miracles.

Prayer of Distress

Are you facing impossible circumstances? It is biblical to made a vow when you are in those difficult situations.

Go to God and say, Lord, here’s what l’m facing. l need this to be solved in my life. And here is what l’ll do.” make a commitment. tell God the amount you are promising. This is not a seed. This is not an offering. There is a difference between a vow and an offering or seed. A vow gets God’s attention in a way that a seed or offering canot. People make vows when they are in danger or face impossible situation. And when they make vows to God, He will listen.
